Department of Computer Science

Chicago, IL

I’m a Computer Science PhD student at Loyola University Chicago, co-advised by Dr. Samie Tootooni and Dr. Dmitriy Dligach, and a member of TRAIL, the Laboratory for Clinical and Translational AI. I work on clinical natural language processing and large language models, specifically those that can translate well into real clinical workflows.

My research focuses on two areas:

  • Multimodal EHR modeling. Combining structured EHR data with clinical notes for outcome prediction. My current work on MIMIC-IV compares early, intermediate, and late fusion for 30-day post-discharge mortality.
  • Prehospital stroke triage. Machine learning on emergency medical services (EMS) data to identify stroke and severe stroke before hospital arrival, combined with implementation science and cost-effectiveness work on what it takes to use these models in the field.

Before the PhD, I earned an MS in Bioinformatics and a BS in Biology at Loyola.
